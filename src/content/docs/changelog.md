The playbook is versioned as a whole. Individual steps will keep getting refined as people actually use them — this page tracks what changed and when.

## v1.1 — 2026-09-07

- Added **11 · Feedback Loop** (Launch phase) — picking a channel for post-launch feedback (a plain form, or a FOSS/hosted board tool like Fider, Astuto, ClearFlask, Quackback, or Canny) and a triage routine for turning it into future work.
- Added a **Research toolkit** section to **01 · Problem & Opportunity** — free/manual research routes (Reddit, Product Hunt, Show HN, app store reviews, competitor feedback boards, F5Bot) alongside paid audience-research tools (GummySearch, Leadverse, OpenScout, Buska, WorthBuild).
- Added a **Validating the flow** section to **03 · UX** — Quant-UX for free clickable-flow testing/analytics, plus posting to r/AlphaandBetaUsers or r/SideProject for outside feedback.
- Added a **Tools for iterating on the look** section to **04 · UI & Design System** — pen.dev, OpenPencil, Claude Design, and Google Stitch for iterating on visual direction before committing it to code.

## v1.0 — 2026-09-06

Initial release. Ten steps from problem research to publishing, each with a template and an LLM prompt, adapted and expanded from an earlier personal `product_ideation` workflow:

- **Discover:** 01 Problem & Opportunity, 02 Idea & Pitch
- **Design:** 03 UX, 04 UI & Design System, 05 Prototype _(new)_
- **Build:** 06 Full Build (PRD, architecture, API spec, task list), 07 Testing _(previously an empty template)_
- **Launch:** 08 App Profile _(new)_, 09 Marketing, 10 Publishing _(new)_
